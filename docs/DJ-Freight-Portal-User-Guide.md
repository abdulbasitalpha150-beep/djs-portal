# 1. Welcome

Welcome to the DJ Freight Portal.

# 2. Getting Started

## Logging In

1. Open the portal in your browser.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Click the sign-in button.
4. If you are prompted to change your temporary password, follow the instructions carefully.

## Changing Your Password

If your account requires a password update:

1. Enter your current password.
2. Create a new password.
3. Confirm the new password.
4. Save your changes.

Choose a password that is easy for you to remember but strong enough to protect your account.

## Your Dashboard

After signing in, you will land on the dashboard. This is the home screen for your daily work.

It helps you quickly see:
- what needs attention
- what is moving forward
- what is waiting for review
- what requires follow-up

## Logging Out

When you are done for the day:

1. Open your account menu.
2. Select Sign Out.
3. Confirm if prompted.

Always sign out when using a shared device.

# 4. Daily Workflow

A typical day in the portal usually follows this pattern.

## Recommended Daily Routine

1. Log in and review the dashboard.
2. Check for new leads and recent activity.
3. Review customer and carrier updates.
4. Open any pending approvals.
5. Follow up on open quotes and loads.
6. Update records as work progresses.
7. Review invoices and commissions where relevant.
8. Leave the portal ready for the next day.

## A Practical Example

A typical day might look like this:

- You begin by reviewing the dashboard for urgent issues.
- You notice a new lead that needs follow-up.
- You open the lead, review the notes, and contact the customer.
- You create or update a quote.
- If the quote requires approval, you submit it and wait for review.
- When the load is confirmed, you record the shipment details.
- Once the shipment is complete, you track the invoice and commission status.

This workflow keeps work moving in a clear and structured way.

# 5. Managing Leads

Leads are the starting point for new business opportunities.

## Purpose

Use leads to track potential customers and opportunities that still need attention.

## When to Use It

Use leads when:
- a new prospect is identified
- a customer inquiry is received
- an opportunity needs follow-up
- a prospect is moving closer to becoming a customer

## How to Create a Lead

1. Open the Leads section.
2. Select Create New.
3. Enter the company name and contact details.
4. Add any relevant notes.
5. Choose the current status.
6. Save the lead.

## How to Update a Lead

1. Open the lead record.
2. Review the current details.
3. Update the status if the opportunity has changed.
4. Add notes about the latest conversation or next action.
5. Save your changes.

## Best Practices

- Keep notes clear and specific.
- Update the lead after each meaningful interaction.
- Use follow-up dates to avoid losing momentum.
- Do not leave leads without a clear next step.

## Things to Avoid

- Leaving a lead without a status update
- Using vague notes such as “follow up later” without detail
- Creating duplicate records for the same company

# 9. Managing Loads

Loads are the operational heart of the portal.

## Purpose

Use loads to manage freight shipments from planning through completion.

## The Shipment Lifecycle

A load moves through several stages:

1. Draft or planning stage
2. Quote review or booking stage
3. Dispatch preparation
4. In transit movement
5. Delivery completion
6. Billing and payment follow-up

## What You Will Manage

- pickup information
- delivery information
- shipment details
- pricing and margin information
- documents related to the load
- current shipment status

## Best Practices

- Keep load details updated as the shipment progresses.
- Review load status regularly.
- Make sure documents are attached when required.
- Keep the customer and internal teams informed.

## Things to Avoid

- Leaving a load in an old or vague status
- Forgetting to update delivery or payment progress
- Letting shipment details become inconsistent across records

# 10. Approvals

Approvals help maintain control and quality across the business.

## Why Approvals Exist

Approvals make sure important actions are reviewed before they move forward. This protects the business, improves consistency, and reduces mistakes.

## When Approvals Appear

Approvals may appear when:
- a new lead is submitted
- a quote needs review
- a record changes in a significant way
- a higher level of review is required

## How to Respond to an Approval

1. Open the Approvals section.
2. Review the request.
3. Decide whether to approve, reject, or request changes.
4. Add a short note if needed.
5. Submit your decision.

## What Happens Next

- If approved, the action moves forward.
- If rejected, the request is returned for correction.
- If changes are requested, the original submitter can update the record and resubmit.

## Best Practices

- Review approvals promptly.
- Use clear notes when requesting changes.
- Keep the process moving without unnecessary delay.

# 15. User Roles

Different users in the portal have different responsibilities.

These users oversee the overall system and manage the broader business operation.

They typically:
- review major activity
- manage user access
- oversee important business decisions
- support the wider team

## Operations Managers

These users focus on the day-to-day flow of work.

They typically:
- monitor activity
- oversee approvals
- keep operations moving smoothly
- help resolve issues quickly

## Team Managers and Lead Agents

These users guide smaller teams and help coordinate work.

They typically:
- review team activity
- support team members

## Agents

Agents work directly with leads, customers, quotes, and shipments.

- follow up with opportunities

## Accounting

Accounting users focus on financial visibility.

They typically:
- review invoices

# 16. Frequently Asked Questions

## I forgot my password.
Contact your administrator or use the password recovery option if available.

## Why can’t I edit this record?
You may not have the right access for that record, or the record may be waiting for review.

## What happens after I submit an approval?
The request is reviewed by the appropriate person and then either approved, rejected, or returned with changes.

## How do I know a shipment is complete?
The load status will reflect the completed stage once the shipment has reached its final step.

## Why is a lead still open?
It may still be active, waiting for follow-up, or awaiting review.

## How do I know which follow-up is urgent?
Check the follow-up list and prioritize anything due soon or overdue.

## Why is a quote waiting for approval?
Some quotes require review before moving forward.

## Can I update a customer record after it is created?
Yes. Records should be kept current as details change.

## What should I do if I cannot find a record?
Try the search tool or review whether the record is in a different status or section.

## How often should I review notifications?
At least once a day, and more often if you are handling time-sensitive work.

## What if I submit a record by mistake?
Contact the appropriate team member or administrator so the record can be corrected.

## Why am I seeing old information?
Some records may have changed recently; review the latest notes and status before acting.

## How do I know whether a load is on track?
Review the current load status and any recent updates attached to the record.

## What if an invoice is overdue?
Review the invoice details and follow up promptly with the relevant party.

## Why is a commission not showing yet?
It may still be pending or linked to a load that has not yet completed the relevant stage.

## What should I do if a customer changes contact details?
Update the customer record immediately so future communication remains accurate.

## Can I create a quote without a customer record?
In many cases, it is best to confirm the customer information first, but the portal may still support the workflow depending on the situation.

## How do I know whether I should create a new lead or update an existing one?
If the company already exists in the system, update the existing record instead of creating a duplicate.

## What happens if I ignore a pending approval?
Work may remain blocked until the approval is completed.

## Who should I contact for account issues?
Contact your administrator or team lead.

## How should I use this portal every day?
Use it as your main place for current work, follow-up, and visibility into the business.

# 18. Troubleshooting

## I cannot sign in.
Check your email and password. If the issue continues, contact your administrator.

## I do not see a menu item.
You may not have access to that part of the portal right now.

## A record is not updating.
Refresh the page and check whether the record has changed. If the issue remains, contact support.

## I am not receiving notifications.
Check your notification area and confirm that the alert is not already visible.

## I cannot find a record.
Use the search tool and check the status or section where the record should appear.
